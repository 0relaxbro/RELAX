## What is RELAX

RELAX is a Solana-based community token built around one simple idea:
markets are chaotic, but your decisions don't have to be.

The story: RELAX survived it — not by ignoring the risk, but by
already pricing it in. He was there when the world ended, and made it
through when others gave up. Now he's here to share that calm, not
advice.

## What's live

- **RELAX Score** — reads a public wallet's recent on-chain activity
  and estimates a FOMO / Panic / RELAX read, with a shareable result
  card. No wallet connection, no signing, no cost.
- **RELAX Swap** — swap any Solana token, built on Jupiter's Swap V2
  Meta-Aggregator. 0% RELAX platform fee, for everyone, always. Every
  transaction is signed by your own wallet — RELAX never holds,
  custodies, or has access to your funds at any point.
- **RELAX NFT Launchpad** — create and launch NFT collections on
  Solana mainnet. Available collections can be minted by eligible wallets
  under their published phase rules.
